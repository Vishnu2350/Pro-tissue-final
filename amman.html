Three generations, one recipe book

Sri Amman Snacks started as a single stove in a Tirunelveli kitchen in 1962. Today the same recipes — unchanged, untouched by preservatives — travel across India to reach your doorstep.

We fry in small batches, pack the same day, and never hold stock longer than a week. If it isn't fresh enough for our own family, it doesn't leave the kitchen.
